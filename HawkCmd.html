Design checklist

Segmentation boundariesSeparate users, servers, guest, voice, IoT, and management where risk or traffic patterns justify it.
Layer 3 gateway placementChoose SVI or routed-interface gateways and document where inter-VLAN routing occurs.
Trunk allow-listsPermit only required VLANs on trunks; avoid relying on the default VLAN for management.
DHCP relay and DNSDocument helper addresses, scopes, reservations, DNS, NTP, and IPAM ownership.
Security policyDefault-deny between segments; add explicit, logged exceptions for required flows.

Operational guardrails

Use only on systems and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to administer.
Change controlRecord a ticket, scope, maintenance window, rollback plan, and approval before changes.
EvidenceCapture timestamps, command output, device identity, software version, and relevant logs.
Credential safetyDo not store passwords, private keys, preshared keys, tokens, or production secrets in field notes.
